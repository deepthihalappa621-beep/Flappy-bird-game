## Introduction

**Flappy Bird** is a simple and addictive arcade game developed by Dong Nguyen. The player controls a small bird and tries to fly through gaps between green pipes without hitting them.

## How the Game Works

1. The bird continuously falls due to gravity.
2. The player taps the screen or presses a key to make the bird flap upward.
3. Pipes move from right to left across the screen.
4. The player must guide the bird through the gaps between the pipes.
5. Each successful pass through a pipe gap earns **1 point**.
6. The game ends if the bird hits a pipe or the ground.

## Main Components

### 1. Bird

* Controlled by the player.
* Moves upward when the player taps.
* Falls downward due to gravity.

### 2. Pipes

* Obstacles that appear continuously.
* Have a gap for the bird to pass through.
* Move toward the bird from the right side of the screen.

### 3. Score System

* Increases when the bird passes a pipe.
* Displays the player's current score.

### 4. Game Over Screen

* Appears when the bird crashes.
* Shows the final score.
* Allows the player to restart the game.

## Features

* Simple one-touch controls.
* Endless gameplay.
* Increasing challenge as the player continues.
* Score tracking.
* Lightweight and fast performance.
